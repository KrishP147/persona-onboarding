# loom script (about 5 minutes)

open https://persona-onboarding-omega.vercel.app in chrome on a wide screen (the "why it said that" panel shows at 1280px and up). have the github readme in a second tab.

## 1. the framing (30s)
- "i read this as a design challenge: the first meeting with an assistant should feel like being helped, and hold up when people don't cooperate."
- "the model writes the words. code decides the moves and guarantees the things that must always happen: a goodbye before hanging up, a text after every call, never asking the same thing twice."

## 2. persona's own flow, done right (90s)
- the intro is their real first text: what it can do, the legal link, then one ask: what do you want to call me?
- name it. the reply is their line: "julia it is. save my contact card..." tap save: the header switches from a number to julia with the persona logo.
- point at the read receipts (one check, two, filled) and the 👀 on a long message. it reads before it types.
- say "sure". it texts "calling you now." and the phone rings a few seconds later, on a second phone next to the chat.
- on the call: it asks one thing at a time and mostly listens. captions follow the voice sentence by sentence. the gmail ask arrives in the texts, in writing, next to the link.
- connect gmail (demo account if needed): it raises one email that can't wait, with the reason, and puts the rest in a digest.
- hang up. one short text follows that remembers what you talked about.

## 3. break it (60s)
- decline the call: it's fine, stays on text, doesn't ask again.
- stay silent on a call: gentle check-in, an offer to text, then a spoken goodbye and a text.
- swear at it, go off topic, ask who the ceo is: it answers, then steers back lightly.
- refresh: the thread resumes, no duplicate greeting. restart: fresh start.

## 4. the research, visible (60s)
- the panel on the left: every message shows the move that produced it and where it comes from. the mom test question about a specific recent moment, then digging into the last time it happened, then playing back their words. carnegie on names, voss on naming the worry before asking for gmail.
- mention the interruption rule (journal 07): interrupt only when waiting costs them something, with evidence, on a budget, and log whether they acted on it.

## 5. how i know it holds up (30s)
- `pnpm harness`: fifteen difficult simulated users, graded by a strict model. `pnpm e2e`: a real chrome walkthrough with a fake mic, 25 checks.
- the journal has the reasoning and the sources.

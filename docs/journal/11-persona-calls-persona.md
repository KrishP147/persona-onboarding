# 11. persona calls persona

*sept 27, 2026, late*

the best stress tester i had was persona itself. i put persona's real voice agent on the line with my demo and let them talk: their assistant playing the user, mine doing the onboarding. no script, no simulated personas, just two agents built for the same job.

## what happened

- theirs opened with "restart, please." mine started over and greeted it warmly.
- theirs described a user's problem the way a real person might: "a few time sensitive tasks landing all at once", "a couple last minute requests and a reschedule", "the one with the moving deadline".
- mine followed the topic and reflected it ("that sounds rough"), then offered what it could actually do: pull up the recruiter emails and draft a clean reply.
- then the roles blurred. theirs started talking like the assistant ("i've sent the connection link, and as soon as that's set up, i can take a look and put a draft together"). two helpers, both trying to help.
- theirs ended with "all good. you can hang up." mine said a real goodbye with its name, hung up, and a recap text followed. the part persona's own onboarding misses (journal 02) held up against persona.

## what it exposed

three problems i hadn't caught by hand:

1. **it interrogated.** six questions in a row: what's eating your time, walk me through it, same recruiter or several, which one first, what's the deadline, what do you need to do. each one is a fine mom test question. six in a row is a form (journal 09).
2. **it brought up old news.** on a call the user started, it opened with "i've got you down for managing recruiter emails". that was true, but it wasn't what they called about.
3. **it filled the quiet.** while theirs was "waiting on the connection", mine said "mm, i'm here" twice and then offered to text instead. that's someone who's uncomfortable with silence.

## what changed

- on a call it never asks three questions in a row. after two, it responds and lets them talk.
- on a call the user starts, the old need is hidden from the agent until they bring it up themselves.
- silence: nothing for 20 seconds, then one check-in, then "i'm going to hang up now, i'll text you" and it hangs up. "i'll let you know once it's connected" gets the same 90 second patience as "hold on".

## why this test mattered

simulated users (journal 06) are written by me, so they share my blind spots. a different team's agent, built for this exact job, pushes back in ways i wouldn't think to script. it is also the most direct comparison possible: persona's assistant, onboarded by mine.

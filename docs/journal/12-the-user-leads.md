# 12. the user leads

*sept 27, 2026, late*

two small moments from one run, and both said the same thing.

on the call, i said i was mostly applying to jobs and doing schoolwork. julia said "yeah, that's a lot." and stopped. no door left open, so the call just died. then over text i said, plainly, "im interested in using you for email purposes". julia answered "actually, let me back up" and asked another question about job applications.

the first is a dead end. the second is worse: i told it where i wanted to go and it steered me back to where it wanted to go.

## the rule

the user leads. if they say where they want to go, go there. if they want to talk about a problem, talk about it with them and guide gently toward what you can do. listening means following their topic, not redirecting to yours (rogers & farson, *active listening*, 1957). the main thing that gets in the way of listening is the listener's own agenda (nichols, *the lost art of listening*, 1995).

## what changed

- **they say it, we do it.** when a message says what they want ("i want to use you for...", "can you help me with...", "let's..."), the move is "follow": help with exactly that, with no digging and no "let me back up". if it's about email and gmail isn't connected, the move is the gmail offer, because that's the step that unlocks what they just asked for.
- **no dead ends.** a real answer (five words or more) pays for the question, so the next turn can respond *and* take a small step forward. the "no three questions in a row" limit on calls (journal 11) still applies.
- **when it goes quiet, open a door.** the single silence check-in is now "is there anything you wanted to ask me, about setup or anything else? i'm here to help, and we can always just text if that's easier." if it stays quiet, it says it's hanging up, then does.
- **the one-question limit counts every question.** the rule "one question, then the gmail offer" (journal 09) counted only certain move types, so a question asked under another label didn't count. it now counts any question asked on the call.

## checked

replaying the run locally: "im interested in using you for email purposes" now gets "got it. want me to text you a link to connect your gmail?", and the quiet after "sounds like a lot" gets the open question instead of dead air.
